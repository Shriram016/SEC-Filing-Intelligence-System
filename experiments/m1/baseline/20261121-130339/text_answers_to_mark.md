**Question:** Why could the EPA's proposed rule on PFAS substances create costs for FedEx, according to its 2023 annual report?

**Gold:** The proposed rule would classify certain PFAS as hazardous substances under CERCLA. PFAS are used in the firefighting foams approved by the FAA for fuel fires at airports, so airports, airlines and refineries could become liable for cleanup of historical PFAS contamination; FedEx could incur remediation costs for its current and former facilities.

**Model:** In calendar 2022 the EPA proposed regulations to categorize certain PFAS as 'hazardous substances' under CERCLA. Once finalized, the rule could subject airports, airlines, and refineries to potential liability for cleanup of historical PFAS contamination (PFAS are used in FAA-approved firefighting foams), so FedEx could incur remediation costs for currently and formerly operated facilities. The impact cannot currently be predicted, and FedEx is committed to transitioning to PFAS-free fire suppression materials per the FAA's May 2023 framework.

## dev_0024 (explain_why)

**Question:** Why does Walmart not record gift card purchases as sales when the card is bought?

**Gold:** A gift card purchase is not a sale until the card is redeemed and the customer buys merchandise with it. Walmart estimates balances that will never be redeemed and recognizes that revenue in membership and other income over the expected redemption period.

**Model:** Walmart does not recognize gift card purchases as sales at the time of purchase because revenue is only recognized when the card is redeemed and the customer purchases merchandise (or services) using it. Unredeemed balances are estimated by management and recognized as revenue (in membership and other income) over the expected redemption period (earlier filings: when likelihood of redemption is remote).
